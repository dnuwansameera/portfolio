About Me

A little about my background, skills, and goals.

D. Nuwan Sameera is a BICT undergraduate and aspiring full-stack web and software developer at South Eastern University of Sri Lanka. I enjoy building responsive, user-friendly web experiences and practical software solutions using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, C, Python, and SQL.

My technical interests include front-end web development, responsive UI design, programming fundamentals, and system automation. Through academic and personal projects, I have developed an Apartment Management System in C, an interactive programming-learning website, and a custom Python voice assistant capable of speech recognition, Sinhala and English speech output, and local task automation.

Alongside technology, my diplomas in Human Resource Management, English, and Psychology have strengthened my communication, teamwork, and people-oriented problem-solving skills. I am seeking internship and junior developer opportunities where I can contribute to real projects, learn from experienced developers, and grow into a reliable full-stack professional.
